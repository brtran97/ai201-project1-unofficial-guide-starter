Happiness is a Cup of Yogurt
Welcome to YoloBerry Yogurt! Get ready to enjoy gourmet frozen yogurt crafted from the highest quality ingredients. You'll want to savor every deliciously creamy bite. Choose from a selection of taste-bud-altering flavors packed with protein and vitamins, including a dairy-free option, and finish it off with toppings that range from fresh fruits to delightful treats. Whether you select traditional vanilla, Dole Whip, or chocolate, topped with sweet strawberries or classic candy, we have something for everyone.

We've been serving one-of-a-kind yogurt to the Davis community since 2008 in our downtown location and are excited to be on campus, catering to Aggies. Swing by the Silo and treat yourself to a yogurt experience. We look forward to serving you!
