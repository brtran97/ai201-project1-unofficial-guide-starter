The Brake in Your Day
Welcome to Spokes! We are your go-to quick-service restaurant on campus, located within the bustling Silo area. We pride ourselves on having service as fast and efficient as a well-oiled bicycle so we can work with your busy schedule. Our menu is made with you in mind and filled with delicious and affordable options that give you the ultimate comfort while studying. Customize your burger, sauce and toss your tenders, add fries and a milkshake and try one of our LTOs (Limited Time Offers) before they're gone.

Our ingredients are always high-quality, and our menu is thoroughly inspired by the bicycles that are so beloved on this campus. For events, you can find us partnering with student organizations and clubs to show our campus pride. So when you’re next craving some loaded fries or crispy chicken tenders, ride on over to us. We look forward to serving you!
