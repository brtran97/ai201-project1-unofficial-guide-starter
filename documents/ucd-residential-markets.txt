Residential Markets: Swing By Our One-Stop Shop
We’ve got you covered at our 3 residential markets—Segundo, Tercero and Cuarto. Our markets have a variety of snacks, beverages, grab-and-go meal options and even testing supplies. We are also dedicated to listening closely to your feedback and continuously evaluating our offerings to keep in stock what is most wanted.

Each market focuses on specific treats. Segundo Market features baked goods and ice cream. Tercero specializes in smoothies, and Cuarto stocks best sellers. With quarterly raffles, upbeat music and even seasonal decorations, the markets provide a stress-free place for you to quickly get what you need. Aggie Cash and EBT are accepted at all markets. We look forward to seeing you!

Cal Fresh
CalFresh is accepted here.
Visit the CalFresh webpage to learn more about CalFresh (SNAP/EBT), including eligibility, items that may be purchased, and participating campus locations.

CalFresh benefits cannot be used to purchase hot food or Peet's Coffee.

If you need assistance, please visit Aggie Compass Basic Needs Center.
