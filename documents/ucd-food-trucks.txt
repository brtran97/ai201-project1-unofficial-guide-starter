Food Trucks
Food trucks serve a variety of food at the Silo

UC Davis Dining Services provides mobile food trucks or carts. View the food trucks locations map (pdf) for specific locations. Trucks will vary weekly, so check the schedule to see this week's truck lineup.

Food trucks accept credit cards and AggieCash, which is available for purchase.
