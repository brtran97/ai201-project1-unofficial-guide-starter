Coffee at UC Davis
At UC Davis, we know you need proper fuel to make it through your class or work day, so we partner with Peet’s Coffee to provide delicious, high-quality beverages. We have two Peet’s Coffee stores, and we proudly pour Peet’s Coffee at four locations. Whether you’re stopping by between classes or staying awhile to study or chill with friends, we’ve got the carefully crafted coffee, tea and tasty pastries that you need. With convenient locations all around campus, allergy-friendly options and a piping hot Cafe All Day menu, we will give you the power you need to have a successful day. So stop by and see for yourself why we love Peet’s so much.

Peet's Loyalty Card
Get your punch card and participate in Peet's Loyalty Card. Simply buy ten drinks and get the eleventh free! There is no expiration date for the Peet's Loyalty Card, and it includes all drink sizes.
