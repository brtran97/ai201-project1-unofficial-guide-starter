Segundo Dining Commons
Segundo Dining Room is one of the most exciting dining venues to reach a university campus. From a brick oven featuring fresh pizza to vegetarian delights and all-time favorite sandwiches and salads, Segundo DC provides students a variety of culinary options for any desire. It's like having several restaurants under one roof!
